Fun cat fact: Cats can "chirp" at birds because their brain pre-plans the bite—the sound is a built-in practice chomp.
Fun dog fact: Dogs sneeze during play to signal they're just having fun and not being aggressive—it's their way of saying "this is still a game!"
Fun octopus fact: Octopuses have three hearts—two pump blood to the gills and one pumps it to the rest of the body.
